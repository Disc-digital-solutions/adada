Buying and registering a used car

Check before you pay, then make the transfer official.

  1. 1Check the name on the registration matches the seller's ID.
  2. 2Check the road tax, insurance and inspection date.
  3. 3Buyer and seller do the transfer together at the road transport office.
  4. 4Get your own insurance before you drive.
  5. 5Cars for sale.

General help, not legal advice. Rules change: confirm each step with the office named.
