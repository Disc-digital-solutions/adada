# Competitors and reference apps

Facts as read from each site or from the cited source on **13 Nov 2026**. Listing counts change daily.
Anything not seen directly is marked **unverified**. Self-descriptions ("the largest…") are the sites'
own claims.

The real competitor is not in this file: it is the **Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp groups** where
people in North Cyprus actually buy, sell and rent. The census in `VALIDATION-KIT.md` measures it.

## 3. What this tells us

1. **Nobody has won.** Seven general sites and three car sites, and the biggest numbers seen are 3,500
   cars on one and 529 on another.
2. **Features are not the gap.** GalerimPlus already has an AI assistant, valuations, auctions and
   bank loans.
3. **Languages are a gap.** Turkish first everywhere; English and Russian on some. **None of the sites
   opened showed Arabic, Persian or French**, the languages of a large share of foreign students.
4. **Nobody joins the verticals.** Each site is one thing: cars, or property, or dorms, or tradesmen.
5. **Mobile is weak at the top.** The property leader's iPhone app has not been updated since 2021.
6. **Charging private sellers earns little.** 529 × TRY 249 per 60 days ≈ TRY 66,000 a month at most.
